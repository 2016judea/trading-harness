Trading Harness

What a business is worth against its price, read off its SEC filings. And what your own trades actually did. In Claude, for free.

https://trading-harness-mcp.vercel.app/mcp

In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, paste the address. No sign-in.

Ask it

“What growth is Coca-Cola’s price asking for, and has it ever done that?”

“Here’s my E*TRADE transactions download. What did I actually make, by stock?”

“Is the stop I wrote down actually resting on my biggest position?”

Six tools

  • As-reported financialsTen years of a company’s 10-K lines, exactly as filed, each with the filing it came from.
  • Owner earnings and reverse DCFBuffett’s owner earnings, return on tangible capital, value anchors, and the growth the price requires. Refuses, with the reason, when there is nothing durable to value.
  • Compare valuationsUp to eight companies ranked on growth the price requires minus growth delivered.
  • Realized P&LPaste a transactions export: first-in-first-out, fees included, win rate and payoff.
  • Stop coverage checkPaste positions and open orders: oversized positions with no stop, orders that die tonight, legs that over-commit.
  • Rebalance order listPaste positions and target weights: the dollar and share change per name, with the tax side of each exit.
It calculates. It does not trade. No brokerage login, no order placed or previewed, nothing you paste is stored. Prices come from you. Not investment advice.
